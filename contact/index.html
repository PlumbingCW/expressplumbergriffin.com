Griffin Express Plumbing is a call-first service. There is no web form to wait on, because a burst pipe does not wait for an email reply. Phone us, describe what you see, and we schedule the first available licensed plumber.

Our dispatch address is 1617 N Expressway, Griffin, GA 30223, on US 19/41 on the north side of Griffin. Plumbers travel to you, so the address is a reference point for the map below and not a walk-in counter.

What to Tell Us When You Call

Where you are

Give the street address and the nearest cross street. If you are outside Griffin, say the town so we can confirm coverage.

What you see

Standing water, a wet ceiling, a cold shower or a gurgling drain all point the plumber to a different tool and a different part.

What you did

Tell us if you shut off the main valve or a fixture stop, and whether the water slowed or stopped.

Access

Mention pets, gates, a crawl space or a locked utility room so the plumber arrives ready.
