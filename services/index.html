Emergencies come first. If water is flowing, start with burst pipe repair. A backed-up line points to drain cleaning or sewer line repair, and a cold shower points to water heater repair.

Planned work is just as welcome. Replacing an old tank, adding a tankless unit or tying in a new fixture are all easier when you call before the old one fails.
