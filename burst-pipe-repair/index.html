If a pipe has burst, find the main shutoff valve and turn it clockwise to stop the flow. The main is usually at the water meter near the street, at the point where the line enters the house, or in a utility room. Then turn off the water heater, open a faucet to drain the lines, and call our emergency line, which is open 24/7.

Burst pipe repair is the emergency response and the fix: stopping the leak, replacing the split section, restoring supply and checking the rest of the system for damage. We schedule a licensed plumber as quickly as the day allows.

The First Ten Minutes

What you do before the plumber arrives limits the damage.

  • Shut the main water valve, or the shutoff for the affected fixture if you can reach it.
  • Switch off power to the area if water is near outlets, panels or appliances. Do it from a dry spot.
  • Open faucets to drain the remaining water and relieve pressure.
  • Move furniture and valuables out of the wet area.
  • Take photos and video of the damage for your insurance claim.
  • Call us and tell us where the water is coming from.

If you cannot find the main shutoff, call us and we guide you through it.

Frozen Pipes in Griffin

Georgia winters bring hard freezes that catch homes off guard. Pipes in attics, crawlspaces, garages and exterior walls are the usual victims. Ice expands and splits the pipe, and the leak often starts only when the ice thaws.

If a faucet gives only a trickle in freezing weather, a pipe may be frozen. Keep the faucet open, warm the area with a hair dryer on low, and never use an open flame. Do not wait for the thaw to find out whether the pipe split. Our water line repair page covers underground lines that freeze or break outside.

Other Causes of a Burst Pipe

Freezing is not the only cause. High water pressure, corrosion, age, shifting soil and a failed connection can all split a pipe. Homes with galvanized steel or polybutylene may see repeated failures, and in that case repiping is the lasting answer.

We find out why the pipe failed so the repair does not just wait for the next break.

What the Repair Involves

The plumber stops the leak, cuts out the damaged section, installs new pipe, restores service and tests for further leaks. We check pressure, look at nearby pipe for damage and tell you what we found. If wall or ceiling damage is present, we point you to the next step for drying and restoration.

Insurance and Documentation

Sudden burst pipe damage is commonly covered by homeowners insurance, though policies differ. Take photos before cleanup, keep damaged items for the adjuster and keep our written report. Call your insurer promptly and ask what they need from us.

Official Guidance for Burst Pipes

The first guidance anyone gives for a burst pipe is the same: shut off the main and keep power away from water. Georgia's state minimum plumbing standard is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), with Georgia amendments published by the Georgia Department of Community Affairs, and local governments can add their own rules.

For a permit question at a Griffin address, call the City of Griffin Permits Division at (770) 233-4130, and for unincorporated Spalding County call Community Development at (770) 467-4280. Georgia 811 marks buried utility lines before any digging, and a licensed plumber arranges the call for excavation work.
