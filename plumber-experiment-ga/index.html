Experiment Housing, Pipes and Septic Questions

Because Experiment is unincorporated, permit questions for most plumbing work go to Spalding County Community Development at (770) 467-4280, not to the City of Griffin. We tell you when a job needs a permit and what the inspector checks.

Some properties outside the city sewer run on septic systems. A septic tank and drain field are a different system from a city sewer line. A backup there can come from a full tank or a failed field, not a clog in the pipe.

If your home is on septic, say so when you call, and we tell you honestly whether the problem is plumbing or tank service. For homes on a sewer lateral, the camera work described on our sewer inspection page applies directly.

Not sure which you have? Look for a capped cleanout and a city sewer bill, or a buried lid in the yard and a service record from a septic contractor. Your county health office can also help identify a system.

After Storms: Why Valve Locations Matter Here

In January 2023 a tornado moved through the Experiment area and damaged the University of Georgia Griffin campus. Storm damage is a plumbing issue too, since broken lines, shifted meters and cracked fittings often show up after the weather clears.

If a storm has hit your property, check the meter box, the main shutoff and any exposed pipe before you restore water. Take photos for your insurer before cleanup. A pipe that looks fine can still leak at a fitting, and a meter that spins with every fixture off is the quickest sign. Our leak detection service finds the source without tearing out walls.
